| D018 | 2026-11-12 | Multi-property loans: the loan's own `<assets>` record carries a `<property>` block that is a roll-up of the whole portfolio, and the individual properties arrive as separate records numbered `1-001`, `1-002`. The clean layer takes loan-level financials from the parent's roll-up block and uses child records only for property attributes. | Observed in BANK 2017-BNK4, January 2024 filing. To be confirmed per depositor family before the clean layer relies on it. |
