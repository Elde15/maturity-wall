| D018 | 2026-11-12 | Multi-property loans: the loan's own `<assets>` record carries a `<property>` block that is a roll-up of the whole portfolio, and the individual properties arrive as separate records numbered `1-001`, `1-002`. Summing all blocks would double-count NOI and value. | D019 | 2026-11-12 | Split notes held in one trust appear as separate asset numbers such as `4A` and `4B`, with no hyphen. The trust's EX-103 explains them. | D020 | 2026-11-12 | Ratios use the whole loan, not the trust's piece. For a pari passu or A-note piece (`loanStructureCode` PP, A1, A2) the property NOI and debt service cover the whole loan, while the balance is only the trust's piece. The clean layer estimates the trust's share as (the trust's scheduled monthly P&I at that root x 12) / (annual debt service from the financials) and divides the trust balance by it (`wl_balance_est`). A share of 0.90 or more, or a `WL` loan with no evidence, is treated as the whole loan. Example: a $30M A-note piece behind $41.6M of NOI implies a $343M whole loan. | Debt yield on the piece alone would read 139% for that loan. Check: after D021, `WL` loans in two test trusts have a median share of 1.00 and PP pieces average 0.40 to 0.44. Cross-trust matching (SOP 8.2) will verify it independently. |
| D021 | 2026-11-12 | "Most recent" financials cover 3, 6, 9 or 12 months. For a partial-year period, some servicers report year-to-date amounts and others report amounts already annualized. The basis is inferred per filing and primary servicer from whole loans, where reported debt service must equal the scheduled payment times the period length (year-to-date) or times 12 (annualized). Partial-year loans with no evidence get `fin_basis = unknown`, no annual NOI, and a flag. | In the two test trusts one servicer annualizes and the other doesn't. A single rule would have doubled NOI for one of them. DSCR is a ratio and is unaffected. |
| D022 | 2026-11-12 | D018 generalized. Six record layouts are resolved per filing and root loan number: `single`, `nested` (one loan record with many property blocks), `parent_children` (loan record with a portfolio roll-up block, plus children with allocated amounts), `replicated` (no loan record; every child repeats the full loan fields, so summing children would multiply the balance), `notes` (split notes sharing collateral) and `orphan`. Each property block gets a role (`property`, `rollup`, `note_block`) so collateral is counted once. Asset numbers follow a tested grammar (`clean/asset_numbers.py`) covering `15`, `15.00`, `06`, `15-001`, `3.01`, `4A`, `6A-1`, `1-A-1`, `15 A-2-C5` and `06A2C`. | The SOP's rule (parent = text before the hyphen) fails on dot children, on hyphenated loan records in the replicated layout, and on note pieces. Example of the replicated layout: GSMS 2017-GS6 reports a 39-property loan as 39 records each carrying the full $72.6M balance. |
| D023 | 2026-11-12 | Percentage units are normalized value by value (`clean/units.py`). Occupancy: 1.0 or less is a fraction, above 1.5 and up to 100 is a whole percent. Rates: 0.25 or less is a fraction, 1 to 25 is a whole percent. Values in the gaps are nulled and flagged. | Units vary record by record, not filing by filing: 2% to 9% of occupancy values and about 4% of rate values in two filer agents' filings are whole percents. |
