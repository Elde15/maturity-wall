| D020 | 2026-11-12 | Ratios use the whole loan, not the trust's piece. For a pari passu or A-note piece (`loanStructureCode` PP, A1, A2) the property NOI and debt service cover the whole loan, while the balance is only the trust's piece. The clean layer estimates the trust's share as (the trust's scheduled monthly P&I at that root x 12) / (annual debt service from the financials) and divides the trust balance by it (`wl_balance_est`). A share of 0.90 or more, or a `WL` loan with no evidence, is treated as the whole loan. Example: a $30M A-note piece behind $41.6M of NOI implies a $343M whole loan. | Debt yield on the piece alone would read 139% for that loan. Check: after D021, `WL` loans in two test trusts have a median share of 1.00 and PP pieces average 0.40 to 0.44. Cross-trust matching (SOP 8.2) will verify it independently. |
| D021 | 2026-11-12 | "Most recent" financials cover 3, 6, 9 or 12 months. For a partial-year period, some servicers report year-to-date amounts and others report amounts already annualized. The basis is inferred per filing and primary servicer from whole loans, where reported debt service must equal the scheduled payment times the period length (year-to-date) or times 12 (annualized). Partial-year loans with no evidence get `fin_basis = unknown`, no annual NOI, and a flag. | In the two test trusts one servicer annualizes and the other doesn't. A single rule would have doubled NOI for one of them. DSCR is a ratio and is unaffected. |
| D022 | 2026-11-12 | D018 generalized.
