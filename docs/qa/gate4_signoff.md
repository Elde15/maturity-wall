## SOP 12 and 13 items checked here

- Property-type templates and confidence tiers as specified. Tier C (lodging, mixed use, other, and any loan without a size measure) gets a value bid and no cash-flow model.
- Execution realism: the bid is labeled an indicative economic bid for the property, not an offer for the loan (`docs/underwriting.md`).
- Los Angeles overrides (SOP 13 step 4): Measure ULA, the City and County transfer taxes, Proposition 13, the City rent ordinance, the County's rent ordinance and AB 1482, each citing its official source and the date it was checked. City limits come from the Census geocoder, not postal names (D058): 222 addresses in the City of Los Angeles, 15 in unincorporated areas. The case study itself is part of the website work.

## What the results say

- The base-case refinance gap is $46.7B across 4,561 of 9,848 valued loans; office holds $24.8B of it. In the downside (rates +100 bp, cap rates +50 bp, KBRA haircuts, 1.40x DSCR) it is $84.2B across 7,609.
- The implied bid sits below the loan balance for 1,794 loans in the base case, at a median discount of 18.6%, and for 3,460 in the downside.
- Negative leverage. Paying market value, the median buyer earns 6.1% unlevered and 2.2% levered, because bridge debt at the 5-year Treasury plus 300 to 425 bp costs more than the property yields. The return bid is below the value bid for all but one loan with a cash-flow model, at a median 75% of it for office and about 80% for the other types.

## Limitations added at this gate

1. Cap rates rest on appraisals at securitization and broker-survey benchmarks, which aren't the same measure: appraisals run below the survey (median spread -0.7 points for office, -1.1 for retail). Results are shown at plus and minus 50 and 100 bp.
2. Rent is spread evenly over occupied space, and only three tenants are listed. Office and retail rollover is approximate (tier B).
3. Proposition 13 uses an estimated seller's tax (the securitization appraisal grown 2% a year) and county average rates.
4. Two choices were changed after seeing results and are disclosed in D057: the spread clip (5th to 95th percentile, then 10th to 90th) and the hurdle component of the opportunity score (a yes/no flag, then a percentile).
5. Three-month financials that the cleaning step's servicer vote marked as already annualized were mostly a quarter's figures (D059). The underwriting inputs correct them loan by loan (266 loans re-annualized); the model features still carry the error on 1.7% of NOI records, which the NOI ablation suggests is immaterial, and the cleaning step will be fixed at the next full rebuild.
6. The hand-check workbook was laid out by a script from the written method, then calculated by Excel. Its formulas are open in the file for any reader to check.
