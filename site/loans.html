Find loans by risk, gap and discount

Every scored loan in the latest filings. Filter, sort, and open any loan for its full tear sheet and the SEC filings behind it. Gaps and bids are in the base scenario.

Probabilities are model estimates (precomputed); gap, bid and discount are the base scenario. Sorted by the column marked with an arrow.

Probability
Failure-to-refinance probability for loans 2.5 to 24 months from maturity (marked F); otherwise the 12-month distress probability (marked D). Rankings are reliable; levels drift between years (method).
Gap
Refinance gap as a share of the balance: what the loan owes beyond the largest new loan a lender would make today.
Discount
One minus the implied bid over the balance. Zero means the property could be sold for enough to repay the loan.
Score
Opportunity score, 0 to 100: the average percentile of risk, gap, discount, returns at market value, confidence tier and size (method).
